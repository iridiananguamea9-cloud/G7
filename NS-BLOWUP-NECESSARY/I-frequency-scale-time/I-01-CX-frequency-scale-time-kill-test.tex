\section{Mission}
\begin{center}
$\boxed{T^*<\infty\Rightarrow C_I}$\\
$\boxed{C_E^{weak}\land C_{L^3}\land C_F^{conc}\land C_I\Rightarrow\bot}$\\
$\boxed{\text{Priority: }W_+\le f(C_I)}$
\end{center}

$T^*<\infty$ strong solution blow-up. $W(t)=\int_{\mathbb R^3}\det(\nabla u)$? or general vorticity/strain interaction. $W_+= \max(W,0)$. Known $C_E^{weak}:\limsup\int_0^t W=+\infty\Rightarrow\int_0^{T^*}W_+=+\infty$, $C_{L^3}:\limsup\|u\|_{L^3}=+\infty$, $C_F^{conc}:\exists t_n\uparrow T^*,x_n,r_n\downarrow0,\|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{r_n})}\ge\varepsilon_0$.

Goal: find $C_I$ frequency-scale-time such that $W_+$ controlled.

\section{Definition $C_I$ attempt}

Let $P_{\le N},P_{>N}$ Littlewood-Paley. Define critical frequency envelope:
\[
N_c(t):=\inf\{N: \|P_{>N}u(t)\|_{L^3}\le \varepsilon_0/2\}
\]
If $T^*<\infty$, define
\[
C_I: \limsup_{t\uparrow T^*} N_c(t)=+\infty \quad\text{and}\quad \int_{T^*-\delta}^{T^*}\|P_{\le N_c(t)}u\|_{\dot B^{1/2}_{2,\infty}}^2 dt <\infty?
\]
Or: there exists frequency cascade: $\exists N_n\to\infty, t_n\uparrow T^*$ with $\|P_{N_n}u(t_n)\|_{L^3}\ge \eta_0$ universal.

Formal proposed:
\[
C_I^{freq}: \exists \eta_0>0\ \text{univ},\ \exists N_n\to\infty,\ t_n\uparrow T^*,\ \|P_{N_n}u(t_n)\|_{L^3}\ge\eta_0
\]

Second variant scale-time: $r_n=N_n^{-1}$ links to $C_F^{conc}$.

\section{Claim $W_+\le f(C_I)$ attempt}

We attempt: $W(t)=\int \omega\cdot S\omega$ (?) decomposition $W=W_{\le N}+W_{>N}+W_{mix}$.

Goal inequality:
\[
W_+(t)\le C \|u(t)\|_{L^3} N_c(t)^{2} + C N_c(t)^{-1}\|\nabla u(t)\|_{L^2}^2?
\]
Or via paraproduct: $\det(\nabla u)$ trilinear. Estimate:
\[
|W(t)|\lesssim \sum_{N_1\sim N_2\sim N_3} N_1^{?} \|P_{N_1}u\|\|P_{N_2}u\|\|P_{N_3}u\|
\]
If $C_I$ forces mass at $N_n$, then concentration $r_n\sim N_n^{-1}$ matches $C_F^{conc}$, but does not yet bound $W_+$ above.

\section{Kill Test}

I-01 KILLED if any holds:

1. $C_I$ is reformulation of $C_{L^3}$: $C_I^{freq}$ follows from $\limsup\|u\|_3=\infty$ without new scale-time info. Then no new bridge.
2. $C_I$ only shows additional concentration: $N_n\to\infty$ equivalent to $r_n\downarrow0$ already in $C_F^{conc}$. No $W_+$ link.
3. $C_I$ reproduces $\varepsilon$-regularity known: smallness of $\|P_{>N}u\|_{L^3}$ implies regularity, i.e., ESS/Seregin again.
4. Critical norm can diverge while $W_+$ diverges arbitrarily: no inequality $W_+\le f(\|P_{\le N_c}u\|,\dots)$; $f$ can be $+\infty$ even if $C_I$ holds.
5. $C_I$ requires hypothesis not derived from $T^*<\infty$: e.g., Type I, $\sup (T^*-t)^{1/2}\|u\|_\infty$, a priori bound on profile, extra Besov bound.
6. No inequality connecting frequency/scale-time info to $W_+$: i.e., $\int_0^{T^*}W_+$ remains compatible with $C_I$.

Check 1-6: At present draft, $C_I^{freq}$ is directly implied by $C_{L^3}$ plus frequency envelope; it does not add control of $W_+$. The trilinear estimate gives $|W|\lesssim \|u\|_{BMO}\|\nabla u\|_2^2$, but $\|u\|_{BMO}$ not controlled by $C_I$ alone. So $W_+\le f(C_I)$ fails.

Hence $C_I$ as defined is not quantitative for $W_+$.

\section{Conclusion}

Thus
\[
\boxed{C_E^{weak}\land C_{L^3}\land C_F^{conc}\ \text{compatible, no }\bot}
\]
\[
\boxed{C_E^{weak}\land C_{L^3}\land C_F^{conc}\land C_I^{freq}\ \text{compatible, no }\bot}
\]
\[
\boxed{C_I^{freq}\ \text{strictly stronger than }C_{L^3}\text{ in frequency, but does not bound }W_+}
\]

Classification: OPEN as frequency-scale-time is genuinely new family distinct from Lagrangian, but KILLED as bridge to $\bot$ since no $W_+\le f(C_I)$ proved.

$\mathcal C_{PROVEN}$ remains $\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3},C_F^{conc}\}$. No new $\bot$.

MISSION: Audit whether $D_t S$ can provide temporal control over $D_t e_i$, closing obstruction from E-07.
INPUT E-07: $D_t e_i \sim \frac{e_j^T(D_t S)e_i}{\lambda_i-\lambda_j}e_j$, $D_t S\supset -\nabla^2 p$, $\mathcal{C}_{PROVEN}=\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3}\}$ frozen.

FORBIDDEN:
$D_t S$ controlled $\Rightarrow D_t e_i$ controlled without spectral gap.
Pressure bound $\Rightarrow$ PROVEN closure.
$C_E^{strong}$ promotion.
Hiding denominator $\lambda_i-\lambda_j\to0$.

AUDIT:

1. Exact identity for $D_t S$:

From NS: $D_t u = -\nabla p + \nu\Delta u$, $\nabla\cdot u=0$.
$\nabla u = S+\Omega$.
Take gradient: $D_t\nabla u + (\nabla u)^2 = -\nabla^2 p + \nu\Delta\nabla u$.

Symmetric part:
\begin{equation*}
D_t S + (S^2+\Omega^2)_{\rm sym} + (S\Omega+\Omega S)_{\rm sym} = -\nabla^2 p + \nu\Delta S.
\end{equation*}
More precisely, writing transport on left:
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{D_t S = -(u\cdot\nabla)S -(S^2+\Omega^2)_{\rm sym} - (S\Omega+\Omega S)_{\rm sym} - {\rm Hess}(p) + \nu\Delta S + {\rm correction}}.
\end{equation*}
With $\Delta p = -\operatorname{tr}[(\nabla u)^2] = -\operatorname{tr}(S^2+\Omega^2)$.

Thus $D_t S$ contains 5 families:
(a) $-(u\cdot\nabla)S$ transport,
(b) $-S^2$ quadratic strain self-amplification,
(c) $-\Omega^2$ vorticity contribution,
(d) $-\nabla^2 p$ pressure Hessian (nonlocal, $p=(-\Delta)^{-1}\operatorname{tr}[(\nabla u)^2]$),
(e) $\nu\Delta S$ dissipation.

2. Control analysis:

(a) $(u\cdot\nabla)S$: needs $\|u\|_\infty$, $C_{L^3}$ gives $u\in L^3$ only, OPEN.
(b) $S^2$: $|S^2|\sim|S|^2$, quadratic, no $L^1_t$ bound from PROVEN.
(c) $\Omega^2$: $|\Omega|\sim|\omega|$, unbounded if enstrophy diverges.
(d) $\nabla^2 p$: Calderon-Zygmund $\|\nabla^2 p\|_{3/2}\lesssim \|\nabla u\|_3^2$, but $S\notin L^3$ from $C_{L^3}$ alone. Nonlocal OPEN.
(e) $\nu\Delta S$: dissipative but no pointwise bound.

Thus $\|D_t S\|$ not controlled by PROVEN.

3. Spectral gap critical:

Even if $|D_t S|\le M(t)$ pointwise,
\begin{equation*}
|D_t e_i| \le \sum_{j\neq i}\frac{|e_j^T(D_t S)e_i|}{|\lambda_i-\lambda_j|}.
\end{equation*}
Need
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{|D_t S|\le M \text{ and } |\lambda_i-\lambda_j|\ge\delta>0}
\end{equation*}
to get $|D_t e_i|\le M/\delta$.

Gap $\delta\to0$ corresponds to eigenvalue crossing, eigenvectors not differentiable, frame singular. No result in PROVEN prevents $\lambda_i-\lambda_j\to0$ near $T_*$. Isotropic $S$ gives small gap.

Hence even with pressure controlled, second obstruction: spectral gap.

4. Classification:

Exact identity for $D_t S$ with 5 terms, each requiring stronger norms than $C_{L^3}$ or $C_E^{weak}$.
Pressure Hessian is nonlocal and quadratic in $u$.
Spectral gap not guaranteed.

OUTPUT:

$D_t S$ structure = $-(u\cdot\nabla)S -S^2-\Omega^2 -\nabla^2 p +\nu\Delta S + {\rm comm}$, EXACT

$\|D_t S\|$ not controlled by $\mathcal{C}_{PROVEN}=\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3}\}$

Even if $\|D_t S\|\le M$, $|D_t e_i|\le M/|\lambda_i-\lambda_j|$ blows up when gap $\to0$

Pressure Hessian $\nabla^2 p$ = Riesz of $u\otimes u$, needs $L^\infty$ or stronger than $L^3$

Result:

(i) $\nabla^2 p$ nonlocal, not in $L^1_t$ from PROVEN,
(ii) spectral gap $|\lambda_i-\lambda_j|\ge\delta$ not guaranteed.

Thus pathway spectral alignment -> control of $W_-$ formally blocked at identifiable point: $D_t e_i$ due to pressure Hessian + eigenvalue crossing.

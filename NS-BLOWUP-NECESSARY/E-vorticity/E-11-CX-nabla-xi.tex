AUDIT:

1. Exact vorticity direction equation:

$\xi=\omega/|\omega|$ where $|\omega|>0$. Then $\omega=|\omega|\xi$.

Vorticity: $D_t\omega=S\omega+\nu\Delta\omega$.

Take $D_t|\omega| = (\xi^T S\xi)|\omega| + \nu\xi\cdot\Delta\omega$.

But $\Delta\omega = \Delta(|\omega|\xi)= \xi\Delta|\omega|+2\nabla|\omega|\cdot\nabla\xi+|\omega|\Delta\xi$.

Project orthogonal to $\xi$: $D_t\xi = S\xi-(\xi^T S\xi)\xi + \nu(\Delta\xi+2\frac{\nabla|\omega|}{|\omega|}\cdot\nabla\xi + |\nabla\xi|^2\xi)$ plus terms preserving $|\xi|=1$.

Key identity for enstrophy diffusion:

$\nu\int |\nabla\omega|^2 = \nu\int |\nabla|\omega||^2 + \nu\int |\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2$.

Thus
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{\nu\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2 = \nu\|\nabla|\omega|\|_2^2 + \nu\int |\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2}
\end{equation*}
Coercive term: $\nu\int |\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2$ appears in dissipation.

2. Link to $W$:

$W=\int |\omega|^2 (\xi^T S\xi)$.

No direct $|\nabla\xi|$ in $W$. But evolution of $\xi$ contains $S\xi-(\xi^T S\xi)\xi$.

If $|\nabla\xi|$ large, vorticity direction highly oscillatory, diffusion large: term $\nu\int|\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2$ consumes enstrophy production. From enstrophy balance:

$\frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_2^2 = W -\nu\|\nabla|\omega|\|_2^2 -\nu\int|\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2$.

Thus
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{W = \frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_2^2 + \nu\|\nabla|\omega|\|_2^2 + \nu\int|\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2}.
\end{equation*}

Compare with original $W=\frac12\dot{}+\nu\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$, consistent.

Now $W_-$? From identity, $W_- \le$? From identity, $W = \text{positive dissipation}+ \frac12\dot{}$, so if $\int W\to\infty$, then $\int \nu|\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2$ may also diverge, but both positive. No sign separation.

Consider: $W_+ - W_- = \frac12\dot{}+D_1+D_2$ where $D_2=\nu\int|\omega|^2|\nabla\xi|^2\ge0$.

Thus $W_+ = W_- + \frac12\dot{}+D_1+D_2$. If $W_-$ diverges in $L^1$, $W_+$ must diverge even more to keep RHS divergence. No contradiction, both may diverge together.

Need lower bound for $W_-$ via $|\nabla\xi|$? Not found.

Thus $|\nabla\xi|$ provides coercive dissipation but not sign separation for $W_-$.

Potential $C_X$ from CF/DHY: If $\|\nabla\xi\|_\infty$ controlled, then $W_-$ bounded? CF: if direction regular in high vorticity region, no blow-up. This uses $|\nabla\xi|$ bounded.

But no $L^1_t$ bound from PROVEN.

\section{Entry rule - 6 steps, kill immediate if unprovable}
1. \textbf{Sequence}: Define $r_n\to0$, $(x_n,t_n)\to(x^*,T^*)$, $u^{(n)}(y,s)=r_n u(x_n+r_ny,t_n+r_n^2s)$, $s\in[-1,0]$. What choice of $(x_n,t_n,r_n)$ is forced by $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{PROVEN}}$? No Type I assumption $r\sim(T^*-t)^{1/2}$. $r_n$ must come from $C_F^{conc}+C_I^{freq}$ only.

2. \textbf{Compactness}: What convergence can we prove for $u^{(n)}$? Aubin-Lions from $C_E^{weak}$ gives $L^2_{loc}$, but need $L^3_{loc}$ to keep $C_{L^3}$? Local energy inequality survives? $p$? Identify exact compactness without assuming bound on $\|\nabla u^{(n)}\|_2$ uniform beyond scaling.

3. \textbf{Limit equation}: Limit $\bar u$ solves ancient NS or Euler? $\nu_n=\nu r_n^{-?}$? If $r_n\to0$, viscous term $\nu_n=\nu$ after scaling? Actually $u^{(n)}$ solves NS with same $\nu$ if $r_n$ scaling is Leray. Euler if $\nu r_n\to0$ not same scaling. Determine precisely.

4. \textbf{Surviving information}: Which of $C_{L^3},C_F^{conc},C_I^{freq},C_J^{neg}$ survives to $\bar u$? $C_{L^3}$ gives $\|\bar u\|_{L^3}\lesssim1$? Or measure? $C_F^{conc}$ gives non-zero? $C_J^{neg}$ gives something about direction? Must be derived, not assumed.

5. \textbf{Rigidity}: Is there independent Liouville theorem killing $\bar u$ given surviving info? E.g., ancient mild NS solution with $\|u\|_{L^3}<\infty$ and concentration $\Rightarrow0$? Known: Escauriaza-Seregin-Sverak $L^3$ ancient $\Rightarrow0$ under $u\in L^\infty_t L^3_x$. Does our $\bar u$ satisfy hypotheses? Need exact statement. No fabrication.

6. \textbf{Nontriviality}: Need $\bar u\neq0$ forced by blow-up assumption. $C_F^{conc}$ should give that, but must be quantitative: $\int_{B_1}|\bar u|^3\ge c_0>0$. If not provable, KILL.

If any step requires hypothesis not in $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{PROVEN}}$ or not provable from $T^*<\infty$ $\Rightarrow$ $\boxed{KILL\ immediate}$.

\section{Step1: centers/scales}
$C_F^{conc}$ gives $t_n\uparrow T^*$, $x_n$, $r_n\downarrow0$ with $\|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{r_n}(x_n))}\ge\varepsilon_0$.
$C_I^{freq}$ gives $N_c(t_n)\to\infty$.
But $\boxed{C_I^{freq}\not\Rightarrow r_n=N_c^{-1}}$ identity, and concentration scale vs frequency scale not proved coincident.
Thus: $\boxed{\text{Step1 = POSSIBLE as candidate sequence, NOT closed as intrinsic scale}}$.

Rescaling: $u_n(y,s)=r_nu(x_n+r_ny,t_n+r_n^2s)$, $s\in(-t_n/r_n^2,(T^*-t_n)/r_n^2)$, window $\to(-\infty,0]$ formally.

\section{Step2: compactness - THE BOTTLENECK}
After scaling:
\[
\|u_n(0)\|_{L^3(B_1)}=\|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{r_n})}\ge\varepsilon_0.
\]
This is lower bound, excellent for $\bar u\neq0$ IF convergence holds.

But we do NOT have:
\[
\|u_n\|_{L^3(B_R\times(-S,0))}\le C(R,S),\quad \|u_n\|_{L^\infty_tL^3_x}\le C.
\]
Leray-Hopf bounds $L^\infty_tL^2$, $L^2_t\dot H^1$ are supercritical under NS scaling $u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u(\lambda x,\lambda^2t)$. No automatic critical uniform bound.

Pressure: $-\Delta p=\partial_i\partial_j(u_iu_j)$ requires simultaneous control of $p_n$ for compactness.

$C_I^{freq}$: $\boxed{\text{has structure at }r_n\not\Rightarrow\text{family precompact at }r_n}$.
$C_J^{neg}$: directional regularity loss, no uniform bound on $u_n,\nabla u_n$.

Thus:
\[
\boxed{\mathcal C_{PROVEN}\not\Rightarrow\text{uniform critical compactness}}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{Leray-Hopf}+r_n\downarrow0\not\Rightarrow u_n\text{ precompact}}
\]
\[
\boxed{r_n\to0\not\Rightarrow u_n\to\bar u}
\]

\section{ZFL verdict}
$\boxed{T^*<\infty\Rightarrow\text{can select }(x_n,t_n,r_n)}$ but $\boxed{\not\Rightarrow u_n\text{ precompact}}$.

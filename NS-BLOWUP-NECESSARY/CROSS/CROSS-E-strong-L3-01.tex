INPUT:
$C_{L^3}$ = PROVEN: $T_*<\infty \implies \limsup_{t\to T_*}\|u(t)\|_3=+\infty$ (ESS).
$C_E^{weak}$ = PROVEN: $T_*<\infty \implies \limsup_{t\to T_*}\int_0^t W = +\infty$.
$C_E^{strong}$ = OPEN: $T_*<\infty \implies \lim_{t\to T_*}\int_0^t W = +\infty$.

$W:=\int \omega^T S \omega$.

MISSION: Audit $C_{L^3}\Rightarrow C_E^{strong}$? or $C_E^{strong}\Rightarrow C_{L^3}$?

FORBIDDEN:
$C_E^{weak}\implies C_E^{strong}$ without extra control (signed $W$ obstacle).
$C_{L^3}\implies C_E^{strong}$ by heuristic growth.
Any contradiction without proven bridge.

Analysis:

1. $C_{L^3}\Rightarrow C_E^{strong}$?

We have $|W|\le C_S\|u\|_3\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$ upper bound only.
No lower bound $W\gtrsim \|u\|_3$ available.
$\limsup\|u\|_3=\infty$ does not prevent $\int_0^t W$ oscillating, even with large negative parts, keeping $\lim$ finite while $\limsup=\infty$.
Hence:
$\boxed{C_{L^3}\Rightarrow C_E^{strong}\ \text{NOT PROVEN}}$.

2. $C_E^{strong}\Rightarrow C_{L^3}$?

Assume $C_E^{strong}$ true: $T_*<\infty\implies \int_0^tW\to+\infty$.
Enstrophy: $\frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_2^2 = W-\nu\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$.
If $\int W\to+\infty$, then $\|\omega\|_2$ blows up (up to dissipation), hence $T_*<\infty$.
But $T_*<\infty\implies\limsup\|u\|_3=\infty$ is ESS, independent theorem, already PROVEN.
So $C_E^{strong}$ does not prove $C_{L^3}$; they coexist under ESS. Logical implication:
$\boxed{C_E^{strong}\Rightarrow C_{L^3}\ \text{NOT PROVEN as implication, but COMPATIBLE via ESS}}$.

Signed obstacle:
$C_E^{weak}$ is $\limsup\int W=+\infty$.
$C_E^{strong}$ needs $\lim\int W=+\infty$.
$W$ changes sign, no control of negative part from $L^3$ alone.
Thus gap remains.

OUTPUT:

$\boxed{C_{L^3}\text{ and }C_E^{strong}\ \text{COMPATIBLE / OPEN}}$

$\boxed{C_{L^3}\Rightarrow C_E^{strong}\ \text{OPEN}}$

$\boxed{C_E^{strong}\Rightarrow C_{L^3}\ \text{OPEN as proof, COMPATIBLE via ESS}}$

No new $C_F$. State remains $\mathcal{C}_{PROVEN}=\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3}\}$, $C_E^{strong}$ OPEN.

Next bridge would require lower bound on $W$ or control of negative stretching.

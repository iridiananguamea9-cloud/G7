Classification:
$C_E^{weak}$ = PROVEN
$C_{L^3}$ = PROVEN
$C_E^{strong}$ = OPEN
MISSION: Audit whether $W_-$ is temporally controlled enough to imply $C_E^{strong}$.
SUCCESS: integrability / domination of $W_-$ => $C_E^{strong}$ PROVEN
FAILURE: no such control => OPEN / BLOCKED
FORBIDDEN: $|W|$ bound => $W_-$ integrable; $W_+$ divergent => cumulative divergent; ESS => $E^{strong}$ without bridge.

Setup:

$W(t):=\int_{R^3}\omega^T S\omega\,dx$ signed.
Decompose $W(t)=W_+(t)-W_-(t)$, $W_\pm(t)\ge0$ pointwise in time via positive/negative part of density, or $W_+=\max(W,0)$, $W_-=\max(-W,0)$.

From $C_E^{weak}$ PROVEN:
$T_*<\infty \implies \limsup_{t\to T_*}\int_0^t W(\tau)d\tau = +\infty$.

Since $\int W = \int W_+ - \int W_-$,
$\limsup\int W = +\infty$ implies
\begin{equation*}
\int_0^{T_*} W_+(t)dt = +\infty.
\end{equation*}
Indeed, if $\int W_+$ were finite, $\limsup\int W\le \int W_+ <\infty$, contradiction. So $W_+$ non-integrable is consequence of $C_E^{weak}$.

Goal for $C_E^{strong}$:
$\lim_{t\to T_*}\int_0^t W = +\infty$.

Write $\int_0^t W = \int_0^t W_+ - \int_0^t W_-$.

Obstacle is $\int_0^t W_-$.

Target controls:

(A) Strong:
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{\int_0^{T_*} W_-(t)dt <\infty}
\end{equation*}
Then $\int_0^t W \to +\infty$ because $\int W_+\to+\infty$ and $\int W_-$ bounded.

(B) Weak domination:
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{\int_0^t W_-(\tau)d\tau = o\!\left(\int_0^t W_+(\tau)d\tau\right),\ t\to T_*}
\end{equation*}
Then $\int W = (1-o(1))\int W_+ \to +\infty$.

Hence if (A) or (B) proven, $C_E^{strong}$ PROVEN.

Why current bound insufficient:

We have $|W|\le C_S\|u\|_3\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$.
Thus $0\le W_-\le |W|\le C_S\|u\|_3\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$.
Near $T_*$, $\|u\|_3$ may blow up in limsup (ESS) and $\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$ is expected to be non-integrable if enstrophy blows up. Product bound gives upper bound diverging, not integrable control.
So:
$\boxed{|W|\text{ bound does NOT imply }W_-\in L^1_t}$.

Similarly, ESS $T_*<\infty\implies\limsup\|u\|_3=\infty$ does not control $\int W_-$.

Thus $W_-$ temporal control is independent hypothesis.

Result of audit:

From hypotheses $\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3}, |W|\le C_S\|u\|_3\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2\}$:

$\boxed{\int_0^{T_*}W_-<\infty\ \text{ NOT PROVEN}}$

$\boxed{\int W_- = o(\int W_+)\ \text{ NOT PROVEN}}$

Therefore:

$\boxed{C_E^{strong}\ \text{remains OPEN / BLOCKED by }W_-\text{ control}}$

No contradiction, no implication fabricated.

State remains $\mathcal{C}_{PROVEN}=\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3}\}$.

Next step would require new estimate: e.g., bound on negative part of strain $\|S_-\|$ or geometric depletion that makes $W_-\ll W_+$ near blow-up.

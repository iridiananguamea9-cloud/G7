Enstrophy identity (where valid):
$\frac12 \frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_2^2 = W - \nu\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$,
$W:=\int \omega^T S \omega$.

Relation proven in CROSS-EF-05-LEMMA-bound:
$|W(t)| \le C \|u(t)\|_3 \|\nabla\omega(t)\|_2^2$.

Assume
$\sup_{t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_3 = M < \infty$.

Then
$\frac12 \frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_2^2 \le (CM - \nu)\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$.

Threshold:
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{CM < \nu \implies \frac{d}{dt}\|\omega(t)\|_2^2 \le 0.}
\end{equation*}

Hence in this regime:
$\|\omega(t)\|_2^2 \le \|\omega_0\|_2^2$.

By E-03 (enstrophy bound => continuation):
$\boxed{\|u\|_3 < \nu/C \implies T_*=\infty.}$

Contrapositive (with same $C$ from estimate):
$\boxed{T_*<\infty \implies \sup_{t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_3 \ge \nu/C.}$

Distinction vs ESS:

ESS gives stronger:
$T_*<\infty \implies \limsup_{t\to T_*}\|u(t)\|_3 = +\infty$.

This lemma gives small-data continuation via enstrophy:
$\|u\|_3 < \nu/C \implies$ no blow-up.

It does NOT replace ESS. ESS is full necessity of $C_{L^3}$.

Classification:
$\boxed{\text{THRESHOLD RELATION PROVEN: } CM<\nu}$

No new $C_F$. State remains:
$\mathcal{C}_{PROVEN}=\{C_E^{weak}, C_{L^3}\}$ in main,

Goal: Determine $C_{\rm quant}$ in $|W|\le C_{\rm quant}\|u\|_3\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$.

1. Derivation exact:

$W:=\int_{R^3}\omega^T S \omega\,dx = \int \omega_i \partial_j u_i \omega_j\,dx$.

IBP (div-free):
$W = -\int u_i (\partial_j\omega_i)\omega_j\,dx$.

Holder:
$|W| \le \|u\|_{L^3}\|\nabla\omega\|_{L^2}\|\omega\|_{L^6}$.

Sobolev $R^3$:
$\|\omega\|_{L^6}\le C_S\|\nabla\omega\|_{L^2}$.

Hence:
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{|W|\le C_S \|u\|_3\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2.}
\end{equation*}
Thus $C_{\rm quant}=C_S$.

2. Conventions:
Domain $R^3$, viscosity $\nu>0$ explicit, norms $L^3,L^2,L^6$ standard.

3. Value of $C_S$:

Sobolev sharp constant (Talenti):
$\|f\|_{L^6}\le C_S^{\rm sharp}\|\nabla f\|_{L^2}$,
$C_S^{\rm sharp} = \pi^{-1/2} \cdot ( \text{explicit}) $; classical bound $C_S\le \frac{2^{2/3}}{ \sqrt{3}\pi^{2/3}}$ etc depending normalization.
We distinguish:
$C_S^{\rm available}$ = explicit non-sharp constant used,
$C_S^{\rm sharp}$ = optimal constant.

For this lemma we keep $C_S$ symbolic, audit says:
$C_{\rm quant}=C_S$ PROVEN, numeric value depends on Sobolev convention.

4. Consequence:

If $M:=\sup_{t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_3$,
$|W|\le C_S M \|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$,
enstrophy: $\frac12 \frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_2^2 \le (C_S M -\nu)\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$.

\begin{equation*}
\boxed{M<\nu/C_S \implies T_*=\infty.}
\end{equation*}

\section{4 gates - all must pass for G10-01}

\textbf{Gate A - Derivable spatial structure:}
Does $T^*<\infty$ + $\mathcal C_{PROVEN}$ imply a spatial property usable for continuation?
Candidates:
- smallness in $L^3$ outside concentration balls?
- $u$ vanishing to infinite order at $T^*$?
- vorticity direction $\xi$ has some $C^\alpha$ patch?
- $u$ coincides with regular solution on open set?
Must be derived, not assumed. If NO: $\boxed{G10=CLOSED/BLOCKED}$.

\textbf{Gate B - Exact hypotheses of continuation theorem:}
Identify theorem: e.g., Escauriaza-Seregin-Sverak backward uniqueness for heat operator $|\partial_t u+\Delta u|\le C(|u|+|\nabla u|)$ with $|u|\le Ce^{C|x|^2}$ and $u(\cdot,0)=0\Rightarrow u\equiv0$; or unique continuation for Stokes; or Carleman for NS vorticity.
List precise bounds required: $u\in L^\infty_tL^q$, decay, vanishing order, differential inequality. If hypotheses require $L^\infty_tL^3$ global small or $e^{C|x|^2}$ or Type I, and we don't have it from $\mathcal C_{PROVEN}$, then $\boxed{BLOCKED}$.

\textbf{Gate C - Transfer from our $C_i$:}
Does $\mathcal C_{PROVEN}$ provide the coefficients in Gate B?
$C_{L^3}$ gives global $L^3$ but not decay $e^{C|x|^2}$.
$C_F^{conc}$ gives $\|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{r_n})}\ge\varepsilon_0$, opposite of vanishing.
$C_I^{freq}$ gives $N_c\to\infty$, not vanishing order.
$C_J^{neg}$ gives loss of $\xi$ regularity, not gain needed for Carleman.
Check: no automatic transfer. If fails: $\boxed{G10=CLOSED/BLOCKED}$.

\textbf{Gate D - Real contradiction with $T^*<\infty$:}
If A+B+C pass, does theorem imply $\bar u\equiv0$ or $u$ extends beyond $T^*$ contradicting $T^*<\infty$?
Must produce $\bot$, not just regularity on open set.

\section{Kill criteria}
KILLED if:
- A requires assuming spatial smallness/analyticity not in $\mathcal C_{PROVEN}$,
- B requires Type I, $L^\infty_tL^3$ uniform smallness, or exponential bound not derivable,
- C cannot bridge concentration (lower bound) to vanishing (upper bound) - they are opposite,
- D only gives partial regularity, not full continuation beyond $T^*$.

\section{Why this is different from G8/G9}
G8: tried $norm\to W_+$ bridge - killed by stretching algebra scale and shear barrier $\bar\alpha\equiv0$.
G9: tried $T^*<\infty\Rightarrow u_n\to\bar u$ ancient - blocked by $r_n^{-1}E_0$ supercriticality and weak limit losing $\varepsilon_0$.
G10: tries $T^*<\infty\Rightarrow$ spatial structure $\Rightarrow$ backward/unique continuation $\Rightarrow\bot$ without needing $W_+$ control nor $u_n$ compactness. Genuinely new class if A holds.

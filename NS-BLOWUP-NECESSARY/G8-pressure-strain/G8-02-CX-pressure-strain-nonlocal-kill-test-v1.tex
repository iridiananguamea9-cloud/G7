\section{Structure of pressure}
$-\Delta p=\partial_i\partial_j(u_iu_j)=\operatorname{tr}S^2-\tfrac12|\omega|^2=\operatorname{tr}(S^2-\Omega^2)$.
Hence $p=\Delta^{-1}\operatorname{tr}(S^2-\Omega^2)$, $\nabla^2p=\mathcal R[S^2-\Omega^2]$ with $\mathcal R$ matrix Riesz transform, bounded on $L^q$, $1<q<\infty$.
So $\boxed{\|\nabla^2p\|_{L^q}\lesssim\|S^2-\Omega^2\|_{L^q}}$.

\section{Test: does $\nabla^2p$ give integrable gain on $W_+$?}
We look for estimate $W_+\le F(\|\nabla^2p\|)$.
$W=\int\omega^TS\omega$. Evolution of $W$ via $D_tS$:
$\frac{d}{dt}W$ contains $-\int\omega^T\nabla^2p\,\omega$.
But $\int\omega^T\nabla^2p\,\omega=\int p_{ij}\omega_i\omega_j$.

Write $\int\omega_i\omega_j\nabla_i\nabla_jp=-\int\partial_j(\omega_i\omega_j)\partial_ip$ up to boundary.
No sign: $p$ not signed, $\nabla^2p$ not coercive.

Nonlocal representation: $\nabla^2p(x)=PV\int K(x-y)(S^2-\Omega^2)(y)dy$.
$K$ is $L^1$-singular, zero mean, no $L^\infty$ gain.
Under $C_F^{conc}+C_I^{freq}$, $S^2-\Omega^2$ concentrates at $r_n\to0$ with $L^{3/2}$-scale critical.
Riesz transform preserves critical scale: $\|\nabla^2p\|_{L^{3/2}}\sim\|S\|_{L^3}^2$.

Thus any bound $W_+\lesssim\|\nabla^2p\|_{L^{3/2}}\|\omega\|_{L^3}^2$ or $W_+\lesssim\|\nabla^2p\|_{L^q}\|\omega\|_{L^{2q'}}^2$ reduces again to $\|u\|_{L^3}\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2$ scaling after Sobolev.
Formally $F(t)\sim\|S(t)\|_{L^3}^3$ or $\|u\|_{L^3}^a\|\nabla\omega\|_2^b$, with $a+b=3$, exactly the dimensional class forbidden as bridge.
Since $C_{L^3}$ allows $\|u\|_3\to\infty$, $\int_0^{T^*}F=+\infty$ compatible with $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{PROVEN}}$.

No cancellation: trace-free gives $\int\operatorname{tr}\nabla^2p=\int\Delta p$, but $W$ uses anisotropic $\xi^T\nabla^2p\xi$, $\xi=\omega/|\omega|$. $C_J^{neg}$ already encodes that alignment is not controlled. Nonlocality does not improve alignment; kernel $K$ is even, does not create negativity for $\xi^T\nabla^2p\xi$.

Pressure as Lagrange multiplier enforces incompressibility, not damping of $W_+$: $D_tS$ term $-\nabla^2p$ is needed to keep $\operatorname{tr}S=0$, not to produce $W_+\le0$.

Therefore no quantitative gain $\boxed{W_+\le F,\ \int F<\infty}$ emerges from pure $\nabla^2p$ structure.

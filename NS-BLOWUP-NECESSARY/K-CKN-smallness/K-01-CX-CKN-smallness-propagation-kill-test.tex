\section{Mission}
\[
\boxed{C_F^{conc}+C_{CKN}\stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}\int_0^{T^*}W_+<\infty}
\]
\[
\boxed{C_E^{weak}\land C_{L^3}\land C_F^{conc}\land C_K\Rightarrow\bot}
\]
where $W_+=\int (\omega^T S\omega)_+ dx$, $C_F^{conc}: \|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{r_n}(x_n))}\ge\varepsilon_0$, $r_n\downarrow0$, $t_n\uparrow T^*$.

Goal is not to assume $W_+\lesssim r_n^{-3}$ by scaling. Goal is to derive
\[
\boxed{W_+(t)\le G(r(t),\varepsilon_0)},\quad \boxed{\int^{T^*} G(r(t),\varepsilon_0)dt<\infty}
\]

\section{CKN barrier}

Standard $\varepsilon$-regularity: there exists $\varepsilon_{CKN}>0$ universal such that if for some cylinder $Q_r(x_0,t_0)=B_r(x_0)\times(t_0-r^2,t_0)$,
\[
r^{-2}\int_{Q_r} |u|^3 + |p|^{3/2} dxdt \le \varepsilon_{CKN}
\]
then $u$ regular in $Q_{r/2}$.

Key: hypothesis is \emph{smallness} of scale-invariant functional. F-01 provides opposite:
\[
\|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{r_n})}\ge\varepsilon_0
\]
i.e., lower bound at time slice, not space-time smallness. No upper bound. Pressure term $p$ not controlled by $F$.

Thus cannot apply CKN directly to deduce regularity at $(x_n,t_n)$. The only information is that outside concentration balls, we might have smallness, but that gives regularity \emph{away} from concentration, not control of $W_+$ which lives near concentration.

\section{Attempted propagation}

Assume we try to get smallness in annulus $B_{2r_n}\setminus B_{r_n}$. If $\|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{2r_n})}\le 2\varepsilon_0$ and lower bound holds in $B_{r_n}$, then outer annulus might have small $L^3$. But $\varepsilon_0$ from $C_F^{conc}$ is lower threshold for concentration, not necessarily $ \ll \varepsilon_{CKN}$. In fact $\varepsilon_0$ could be $\ge \varepsilon_{CKN}$, so even smallness fails.

Even if we had $r^{-2}\int_{Q_r} |u|^3\le\varepsilon_{CKN}$ for $r\sim r_n$, CKN gives
\[
\|u\|_{L^\infty(Q_{r/2})}\le C r^{-1}
\]
Then naive bound:
\[
W_+(t)\lesssim \int_{B_{r/2}} |\nabla u|^3 \lesssim r^{-3} |B_{r/2}| \sim r^0? \text{ Actually } |\nabla u|\lesssim r^{-2}\Rightarrow |\omega|^2\alpha\lesssim r^{-6}
\]
More precise: if $|u|\lesssim r^{-1}$, then $|\omega|\lesssim r^{-2}$, $W_+\lesssim r^{-3}$ after integration over ball $r^3\cdot r^{-6}=r^{-3}$.

Thus $W_+(t)\lesssim r_n(t)^{-3}$. Since $r_n\downarrow0$ as $t\uparrow T^*$, this bound blows up, does \emph{not} give integrability:
\[
\int^{T^*} r_n(t)^{-3} dt = +\infty \text{ unless } r_n(t)\gtrsim (T^*-t)^{\beta},\ \beta<1/3
\]
No relation between $r_n(t)$ and $T^*-t$ is provided by $C_F^{conc}$ or $C_I^{freq}$; only existence of sequence $r_n\downarrow0$, no rate.

To get $\int G<\infty$, need $G(r,\varepsilon_0)=o((T^*-t)^{-1})$. But $r_n^{-3}$ gives opposite.

Hence even if CKN gave local $L^\infty$ bound, it worsens $\int W_+$.

\section{Kill Test}

K-01 KILLED if:

1. F-01 only provides lower bound, CKN needs upper/smallness: we have no $r^{-2}\int |u|^3\le\varepsilon_{CKN}$.
2. CKN only gives local regularity away from concentration, not $W_+$ control near concentration.
3. Local regularity does not yield temporally integrable $W_+$ estimate: $r_n^{-3}$ not integrable as $r_n\to0$.
4. $r_n^{-3}$ appears only by dimensional analysis, without derivation from CKN + $C_F^{conc}$.
5. Estimate still allows $\int_0^{T^*}W_+=+\infty$: compatible with blow-up, no $\bot$.

All 1-5 hold. The attempted $G(r,\varepsilon_0)=C r^{-3}$ fails integrability. No quantitative link $r(t)\sim (T^*-t)^{1/2}$ can be derived from $L^3$ concentration alone; would require Type I assumption, which is not consequence of $T^*<\infty$ (blocked).

Therefore $C_F^{conc}+C_{CKN}$ does not imply $\int W_+<\infty$.

\section{Conclusion}

\[
\boxed{C_K: \exists r(t) \text{ with } r^{-2}\int_{Q_{r}}|u|^3\le\varepsilon_{CKN}\ \text{not derivable from }T^*<\infty}
\]
\[
\boxed{W_+(t)\le G(r(t),\varepsilon_0)\text{ with }G\sim r^{-3}\text{ not integrable}}
\]
\[
\boxed{C_E^{weak}\land C_{L^3}\land C_F^{conc}\land C_K\not\Rightarrow\bot}
\]

Classification: CKN is genuinely distinct family, but K-01 KILLED as bridge. Smallness cannot be extracted from lower bound; even if extracted away from concentration, it does not control $W_+$ near concentration in integrable way.

$\mathcal C_{PROVEN}$ remains $\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3},C_F^{conc},C_I^{freq},C_J^{neg}\}$.

No K-02. Line closed.

MISSION: Audit whether geometric depletion mechanisms provide quantitative control relevant to $W_-$ bottleneck from E-strong-neg-02.
INPUT E-strong-neg-02: $W_-$ temporal control = OPEN/BLOCKED, $W_+\in L^1_{loc}$ insufficient, $\int W_+=\infty$ PROVEN from $C_E^{weak}$, $C_E^{strong}$ requires $W_-\in L^1(0,T_*)$.

EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES TO IDENTIFY:
Constantin-Fefferman (CF93), Deng-Hou-Yu (DHY05/06). Exact theorem version to be verified.

FORBIDDEN:
geometric alignment $\Rightarrow W_-\in L^1_t$ without explicit time estimate.
citation $\Rightarrow$ theorem applicability.
external criterion $\Rightarrow$ internal PROVEN status.

AUDIT:

A. Constantin-Fefferman family:

Exact hypotheses (CF93):
In region of large $|\omega|$, direction $\xi=\omega/|\omega|$ Lipschitz: $|\sin\phi(x,y)|\le |x-y|/\rho$ with $\rho>0$, plus $|\omega|\in L^\infty$ control? Regularity of $\xi$ in high vorticity region.

Exact geometric quantity:
$\rho^{-1}$ = Lipschitz constant of direction, or $|\nabla\xi|$ in $L^\infty$.

Exact conclusion:
If $\|\nabla\xi\|_{L^\infty}$ bounded and velocity bounded in suitable, then no blow-up? Actually CF gives $ \|\omega(t)\|_\infty$ controlled exponentially by $\|u\|_\infty$ times geometric factor.

Whether controls $W,W_-$ or other:
Controls $D_t|\omega| = \alpha |\omega|$, $\alpha = \xi^T S \xi$. Note $W=\int \alpha |\omega|^2$. So geometric regularity controls $\alpha$ pointwise, not directly integral sign split $W_-$.

Scaling:
$\nabla\xi\sim length^{-1}$, critical if $\rho\sim \sqrt{T_*-t}$.

Time-integrability:
CF requires $\int_0^{T_*} \|u\|_\infty^2 dt$? No $L^1_t$ for $W_-$ directly. Estimate: $|\alpha|\le C\|u\|_{Lip}$ but not sign.

Applies near $T_*$? Requires uniform Lipschitz of $\xi$ in high vorticity region up to $T_*$, strong assumption.

Bridge to $C_E^{strong}$?
No direct $W_-\in L^1$. Only gives bound on $|\alpha|$, i.e., $|W|\le C\int |\omega|^2\|u\|_{Lip}$.

B. Deng-Hou-Yu family:

Exact hypotheses (DHY):
Conditions on vortex line length $L(t)$, curvature $\kappa$, divergence of $\xi$, plus $U_\xi$, $U_n$ velocities.

Exact geometric quantity:
$L(t)$ of vortex line segment, $\int_0^{L} (\nabla\cdot\xi)_+$, $M(t)=\max |\omega|$ along line, etc.

Exact conclusion:
If $L(t)\ge c L_0$, curvature and divergence controlled in $L^\infty$, and $U_\xi,U_n$ bounded, then no blow-up up to $T_*$.

Controls $W,W_-$ or other:
Controls growth of $\|\omega\|_\infty$ via Lagrangian stretching of vortex lines: $\partial_t |\omega| / |\omega| \sim$ divergence + curvature terms. Again controls $|\alpha|$, not sign split.

Scaling:
Length $L(t)$ critical if $\sim (T_*-t)^{1/2}$? Actually geometric non-collapsing.

Time-integrability:
Conditions are pointwise in time $L^\infty_t$ bounds on geometric quantities, not $L^1_t$ for $W_-$.

Applies near $T_*$? Yes, designed as non-blow-up criterion up to $T_*$.

Bridge to $C_E^{strong}$?
Would need to extract $W_-=\int (-\alpha)_+ |\omega|^2$. If $\alpha$ bounded above by geometric quantity $G(t)$, then $W_-\le \int G_- |\omega|^2$? But $G$ not sign definite. Still no $L^1$.

OUTPUT:

$\boxed{\text{CF: controls }|\alpha|,\text{ not }W_-\in L^1_t\text{ directly}}$

$\boxed{\text{DHY: controls }\|\omega\|_\infty\text{ growth, not }W_-\text{ integrability}}$

$\boxed{\text{geometric alignment }\not\Rightarrow W_-\in L^1(0,T_*)\text{ without extra temporal bridge}}$

$\boxed{\text{BRIDGE to }C_E^{strong}\text{ = OPEN/BLOCKED: PARTIAL BRIDGE only on }|\alpha|}$

Hence E-strong-neg-02 remains bottleneck: geometric depletion controls magnitude of stretching, not its signed decomposition required for $\int W_+=\infty\Rightarrow C_E^{strong}$.

Potential new pathway: geometric quantity $G(t):=\|\nabla\xi\|_\infty$ or $(L,\kappa,U)$ as independent $C_G^{geom}$ candidate, to be crossed with $C_{L^3}$ and $C_G^{CKN}$, rather than forcing $W_-$ control.

$\mathcal{C}_{PROVEN}=\{C_E^{weak},C_{L^3}\}$ untouched.

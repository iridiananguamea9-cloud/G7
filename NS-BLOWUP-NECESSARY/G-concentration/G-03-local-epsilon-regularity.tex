MISSION: Determine whether external local epsilon-regularity yields exact $C_G$ from G-01.
TARGET G-01 $C_G^{L^3}$: $\exists t_n\uparrow T_*, x_n, r_n\downarrow0: \|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B(x_n,r_n))}\ge\epsilon_0$

FORBIDDEN:
$L^\infty_t L^3(B_1)\Rightarrow$ regularity without theorem match.
CKN citation as substitute for exact implication.
ESS-global $\Rightarrow$ local epsilon-regularity.

AUDIT:

1. Exact theorem search:

Candidate A: CKN $\epsilon$-regularity (1982):
Suitable weak $(u,p)$ in $Q_1:=B_1\times(-1,0)$,
$\mathcal{Q}(r):= r^{-2}\int_{Q_r}|u|^3 + r^{-2}\int_{Q_r}|p|^{3/2}$.
If $\mathcal{Q}(1)\le\epsilon_{CKN}$ universal, then regular in $Q_{1/2}$.
Quantity is space-time + pressure, not instantaneous $L^3(B)$.

Candidate B: Kozono-Taniuchi / ESS local $L^3$:
If $\limsup_{r\to0} r^{-3}\|u\|_{L^\infty_t L^3(Q_r)}^3$ small? Formulation varies.
Most statements require $u\in L^\infty_t L^3_x(Q_1)$ and smallness of $\|u\|_{L^\infty_t L^3}$, plus suitable.

Candidate C: Seregin's $L^3$ epsilon-regularity: $ \|u\|_{L^\infty_t L^3(Q_1)}\le\epsilon_*$ implies regularity, but needs suitable + pressure bound from local energy inequality.

Issue: No theorem found so far of pure form $\|u(t)\|_{L^3(B_1)}\le\epsilon_*\Rightarrow$ regularity at time $t$ without space-time and pressure.

2. Suitable-weak hypotheses:
All CKN-type require $u\in L^\infty_t L^2\cap L^2_t H^1_{loc}$, $p\in L^{3/2}_{loc}$, local energy inequality.

3. Space-time vs instantaneous:
CKN quantity $\mathcal{Q}$ involves $ \int_{-r^2}^0\int_{B_r} |u|^3$, not $\|u(t_n)\|_{L^3(B_{r_n})}$ at single time. So contrapositive gives:
If singular at $(x_*,T_*)$, then $\limsup_{r\downarrow0}\mathcal{Q}(r)\ge\epsilon_{CKN}$.
This is $C_G^{CKN}$, not $C_G^{L^3}$.

4. Scaling:
$\mathcal{Q}(r)$ scale invariant: $|u|^3\sim r^{-3}$, $dxdt\sim r^5$, so $r^{-2}\int |u|^3\sim1$. Matches $L^3$ critical.
$ \|u(t)\|_{L^3(B_r)} $ also critical: scaling $r^{-1}\|u\|_3^3$.

5. Pressure:
$C_G^{CKN}$ includes $p$. Cannot drop without theorem. $C_G^{L^3}$ would require pressure controlled by $u$ via Riesz, not immediate locally.

6. Universality $\epsilon_*$:
CKN $\epsilon_{CKN}$ universal (depends only on dimension). If theorem valid, universal.

7. Does it give regularity at required point/scale?
CKN gives: if singular at $(x_*,T_*)$, then $\mathcal{Q}(r)\ge\epsilon_*$ for all small $r$ (up to rescaling). So produces $r_n\downarrow0$.

8. Exact contrapositive producing $C_G$:

If $T_*<\infty$ and $(x_*,T_*)$ singular point, then for $Q_{r_n}(x_*,T_*)$,
\begin{equation*}
\boxed{\mathcal{Q}(r_n)= r_n^{-2}\int_{Q_{r_n}}|u|^3 + r_n^{-2}\int_{Q_{r_n}}|p|^{3/2} \ge \epsilon_{CKN}}
\end{equation*}
Call this $\boxed{C_G^{CKN}}$.

Thus:
$\boxed{C_G^{L^3}\neq C_G^{CKN}\text{ automatically}}$.

$\boxed{C_G^{L^3}\text{ NOT DERIVED from CKN alone}}$

$\boxed{C_G^{CKN}\text{ PROVEN CONDITIONAL on CKN epsilon-regularity as external dependency}}$

STATUS:

$\boxed{C_G^{L^3}\text{ remains OPEN/BLOCKED: needs local }L^\infty_t L^3\text{ theorem exact}}$

$\boxed{C_G^{CKN}\text{ PROVEN-CONDITIONAL: EXTERNAL-DEPENDENCY: CKN-1982}}$

Hence split:

$C_G^{L^3}$: G-01 candidate, OPEN.

$C_G^{CKN}$: new candidate, PROVEN-CONDITIONAL on CKN.
